01 FOCUS

現場在意的四件事

Four priorities on the floor

01

換輪不延遲

Fast round turnovers

下一輪配對按一下就出來。選手拿自己的手機看桌號與對手，全場幾分鐘內就定位，不會為了換輪把時間一路拖下去。

The next round is paired with one click. Players find their table and opponent on their own phones, so the floor resets in minutes instead of dragging the schedule out.

02

服務台不塞車

Eliminate help desk crowds

QR 自助報到、選手各自用手機回報並雙向確認成績，不一致才進裁判佇列。大螢幕輪播桌況與計時，選手清楚流程，不用一直跑來問工作人員。

QR self check-in and mutual score confirmation on players' phones. Disputes route straight to judges. Big-screen match clocks and table boards keep players informed without crowding staff.

03

當機斷線有防呆

Resilient floor operations

成績填錯隨時可撤銷更正。賽務主機就算意外斷電，重開就接回賽況；網路斷線時頁面會誠實提示，絕不靜默顯示舊資料讓選手空等。

Scores can be rolled back or corrected at any time. If the host loses power, it picks the event back up on restart. When a device drops offline the page says so, instead of quietly showing stale data.

04

直播自動化疊圖

Automated stream overlays

多機位攝影與畫面切換。即時比分、輪次、對戰選手與倒數時間直接從賽務系統餵進畫面，導播專心配賽，不用手忙腳亂人工改字。

Multi-camera scenes and switching. Match scores, round numbers, player names, and clocks sync automatically from the tournament engine — no manual typing during broadcast.

Zanmen Cup (BEYBLADE X Live Tournament)

這場在南科贊美酒店宴會廳舉行的實體賽事規模達 512 人，必須在單一營業日內順暢打完全部賽程。我一人統籌現場整套賽務系統開發與多機位直播導播，確保賽事流程與視訊畫面同步推進。

A single-day, 512-player tournament held in the ballroom of L'ARC Hotel, Tainan. Developed the tournament management system and directed the live multi-camera broadcast simultaneously.

  • 賽務系統： Tournament Software: QR 自助報到、工作人員快速審核、抽籤與上台流程、對戰表排程、成績雙向回報與撤銷更正。 QR self check-in, staff roster verification, draw & stage dispatching, bracket scheduling, mutual score confirmation, and rollback adjustments.

符文戰場 (Riftbound) 賽事系統

Riftbound Tournament System

嚴格依照官方《Riftbound Tournament Rules》架構實作，專為 512 人高併發卡牌大賽 設計。將裁判執法、選手雙向回報、官方同分排名計算法與 OBS 直播疊圖整合進單一輕量引擎。

Engineered strictly according to the official Riftbound Tournament Rules for 512-player competitive card events. Unifies judge operations, mobile mutual result confirmations, official tiebreaker algorithms, and OBS live streaming graphics into a single responsive engine.

  • 賽規引擎： Rules Engine: 瑞士輪配對引擎與官方同分排名演算法；選手用各自手機雙方確認成績，結果不一致才進裁判佇列。 Swiss pairing engine with official tiebreaker calculations. Players confirm match results on their phones; only disputed scores enter the judge queue.
  • 11 個專用畫面： 11 Purpose-built Views: 報到、牌表登錄、找桌與回報、排名、淘汰賽樹、大螢幕計時、桌況板、裁判、主辦控制台、觀眾端、OBS 疊圖，涵蓋全天所有崗位。 Check-in, decklist submission, table lookup & reporting, standings, top-cut bracket, floor timer, table board, judge queue, admin console, spectator view, and OBS overlay.
  • 現場流程管理： Floor Operations: 裁判呼叫排隊系統、牌表登錄與隨機抽查功能；賽後成績一鍵匯出（符合官方賽規強制保存三個月之要求）。 Integrated judge-call queue, decklist registration with random deck checks, and post-event export meeting the mandatory three-month retention rule.

512 人現場併發與容錯實測

Field Stress & Fault Tolerance Tests

  • 按下去就出來： Instant turnover: 512 人下一輪配對秒級產生，全場選手拿起手機就看到桌號與對手，換輪不需全場枯等。 512-player pairing generated in seconds. Players look up table assignments on their phones without floor delays.
  • 全場一起交也不塞： High concurrency: 全場 256 組對戰在最後幾分鐘集中用手機回報勝負與雙向確認，壓力測試一筆都沒漏。 All matches reporting and cross-confirming at the bell; verified with zero dropped entries under peak loads.
  • 當機也接得回來： Crash safe: 賽務筆電就算突遭拔除電源，重開後整場進度立刻原位續跑；斷線時網頁主動提示，絕不靜默掩飾。 If the host laptop loses power, restarting resumes state instantly; client screens clearly signal connection loss instead of freezing on stale data.
